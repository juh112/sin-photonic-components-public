# Evidence and scope

Selected research figures by Jun Young Hong are shared as a technical portfolio. Figures retain numerical annotations needed to understand the examples. Detailed fabrication layouts, full-chip masks, raw experimental archives, process recipes and native commercial-solver projects are not distributed. No fabricated-device performance is inferred from simulation alone.

This is a visual case study, not a complete reproducibility archive. Original numerical runs were not repeated for this publication. No new open-source license is assigned.
